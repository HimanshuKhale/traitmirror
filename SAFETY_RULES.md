# Safety Rules

The app must not say:
- You are lying.
- You are toxic.
- You are a narcissist.
- You have a disorder.

Use safer language:
- Your story may be incomplete.
- Your answers suggest self-protective framing.
- Your responses show a possible avoidance pattern.
- This is a reflection tool, not a diagnosis.

Narrative scoring should measure:
- detail specificity
- timeline consistency
- perspective-taking
- contradiction risk
- disclosure completeness
- self-protective framing

Never claim real lie detection.
