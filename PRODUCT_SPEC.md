# Product Spec

## App Name
TraitMirror

## Core Promise
See yourself clearly. Close the gap. Become who you choose to be.

## Core Modules
1. Ideal Self Builder
2. Scenario Assessment
3. Narrative Consistency Interview
4. Trait Gap Dashboard
5. AI Reflection Report
6. Daily Growth Plan

## Not a Clinical Tool
The app does not diagnose mental illness, personality disorders, or deception.
